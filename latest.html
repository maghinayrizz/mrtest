GLOBAL CUSTOMER SUPPORT: TIPS & TRICKS Issue No. 2

Salesforce: Omni-Channel Logins

Log in at the start of your day, stay available, and keep cases and KPIs on track.

WHAT'S AT RISK Case allocation and customers Missed logins delay case allocation, uneven workloads, hide team capacity and slow response times for customers.
WHAT'S AT RISK Efficiency KPIs Omni-Channel activity is a key indicator of availability. When you're not logged in, individual and team KPI reporting becomes less accurate.
THE FIX: MAKE IT PART OF YOUR DAILY ROUTINE
Log in at the start of your shift Open Salesforce Omni-Channel at the beginning of your working day, before you pick up support activities.
Set your status correctly Make sure your status reflects your actual availability.
Stay logged in all day Remain logged in throughout the day unless you are on a scheduled break, in training, or attending meetings.
Update your status promptly Whenever your availability changes, change your status right away so cases route to the right people.
Your day in Omni-Channel Your status should always match what you're doing right now.
1Start of shiftLog in
2Handling casesStay available
3Break, training or meetingUpdate your status
4Back to workUpdate it again
Why it matters: Consistent use of Omni-Channel ensures cases are distributed efficiently, individual and team efficiency KPIs are accurately reflected, and we maintain the service levels our customers and stakeholders expect. Thank you for your cooperation and support in improving our operational effectiveness.
